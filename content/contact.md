## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do you offer fixed-price projects or hourly rates?**  
A: Both. We typically recommend fixed-price for well-defined projects (e.g., specific SAC widget, dashboard development) and time-and-materials for ongoing optimization or complex migrations where scope may evolve.

**Q: What's your typical engagement duration?**  
A: Varies by project. Quick wins like single custom widgets: 2-4 weeks. Full Datasphere migrations: 3-6 months. We offer flexible engagement models.

**Q: Do you sign NDAs and work under master service agreements?**  
A: Absolutely. We're experienced working with enterprise procurement processes and happy to work within your existing frameworks.

**Q: Can you work with our existing SAP partner or integrator?**  
A: Yes, we frequently collaborate with client IT teams, SAP partners, and other consultancies in a complementary capacity.
