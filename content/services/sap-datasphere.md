## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Should we migrate from BW to Datasphere or stay on BW/4HANA?**  
A: Depends on your strategy. Datasphere is cloud-native with consumption pricing, ideal for cloud-first organizations. BW/4HANA is better for on-premise strategies with complex BW investments. We help you evaluate based on your specific situation.

**Q: Can Datasphere replace our entire data warehouse?**  
A: For many use cases, yes. Datasphere supports both data virtualization (federation) and data persistence (replication). We assess your requirements to determine the right balance.

**Q: How long does a typical BW to Datasphere migration take?**  
A: Varies by complexity. Small BW systems: 6-12 weeks. Medium complexity: 3-4 months. Large enterprises with complex BW: 6-12 months. We provide detailed estimates after assessment.

**Q: Do you support ongoing Datasphere operations post-implementation?**  
A: Yes, we offer retainer-based support for ongoing optimization, troubleshooting, and enhancement requests.
