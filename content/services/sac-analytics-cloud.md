## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can SAC replace our existing BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, BusinessObjects)?**  
A: For SAP-centric organizations, yes. SAC offers native SAP connectivity, integrated planning, and predictive analytics. We help assess based on your specific landscape.

**Q: How does SAC pricing work?**  
A: Subscription-based per user per month. Different tiers (Viewer, Planner, Professional). We help optimize license allocation based on user roles.

**Q: Can we embed SAC dashboards in our applications?**  
A: Yes, SAC supports URL embedding, iframe embedding, and SAP Analytics Hub for seamless integration.

**Q: What's the learning curve for SAC?**  
A: End users: Low (similar to Excel). Power users: Medium (story design). Developers: Medium-High (calculations, scripting). We provide comprehensive training.

**Q: Do you support SAC migrations from BusinessObjects or other legacy BI?**  
A: Yes, we have migration methodologies for legacy BI to SAC, including content assessment, priority roadmap, and phased migration.
